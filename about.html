About

Hello! I am Treasure Fatile, a visual artist based in Toronto and a recent graduate of the Art and Art History program at the University of Toronto Mississauga.

Rooted in traditional painting techniques, my evolving practice incorporates explorations in collage, printmaking, and textile work. Currently, my works seek to understand how different environments influence the roles women assume and the sacrifices they are compelled to make.
